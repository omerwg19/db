About

Exposure is spread across dozens of places

Checking them one at a time is slow, error-prone, and easy to abandon halfway. bugatti.lol does the fan-out and the correlation, so the investigation starts from a picture rather than a pile of tabs.

Correlate, don't just collect

Core

The same person often appears under a different handle, number and address in each source. We normalise records into one schema and deduplicate by field, so one breach in four feeds reads as one finding.

Provenance on every hit

Always

Every result names its originating source and the date the record was observed. If a finding cannot be traced back, we do not show it.

Queries leave no history

By design

What you search is processed in memory and not logged. Investigating a sensitive subject should not itself become a record about you.

Built around lawful use

Policy

Our acceptable use policy is short and enforced. We would rather lose an account than help someone misuse the capability.
